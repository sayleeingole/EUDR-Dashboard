# 05 — Article 9 checklist generation (Sector 3)

Produces the supplier documentation checklist per country/region × cycle. The checklist is **generated from signed conclusions, never hand-assembled**, so every item is traceable to its cause.

## The two streams

### Stream 1 — Legality baseline (from Sector 2)
The bare minimum to substantiate Art. 9(1)(h) legality: the union of verification documents linked to all *relevant* legal requirements for the country/region. Grouped by area of law (A1–A7). Always requested regardless of risk level — legality is not risk-dependent.

Universal Art. 9 items are part of the baseline for every country, including:
- geolocation of all plots of land (polygons for plots > 4 ha), with production date ranges;
- supplier and supply chain identification down to plot level;
- quantities per plot;
- deforestation-free declaration referencing the 31 Dec 2020 cutoff.

### Stream 2 — Risk add-ons (from Sector 1)
Generated by the **mitigation rules** library from each signed section rating. Each rule:

> IF section S has confirmed rating ≥ L (in country/region C) THEN add mitigation measures M and request documents D, justification J.

Seed examples (editable in Admin, versioned):

| Trigger | KLK mitigation measures | Documents requested |
|---|---|---|
| 10(2)(j) mixing ≥ high | Prefer SG/IP chains; quarterly supplier engagement on third-party FFB intake; monitor collection points/dealers | Supply chain model declaration (IP/SG/MB) with justification; mill FFB intake register summary; traceability-to-mill (TTM) and traceability-to-plantation (TTP) documentation; list of CPO origins blended at refinery |
| 10(2)(f,k) deforestation ≥ high | Plot-level screening vs 2020 cutoff; monitor surrounding areas; alert-based escalation | Geolocation polygons incl. plots < 4 ha where feasible; land-use change analysis / satellite screening report; licences and titles for expansion areas |
| 10(2)(l) human rights ≥ high | Screen against WRO/TVPRA listings; require labour compliance evidence; monitor grievance channels | Labour law compliance declaration; recruitment fee policy; evidence of grievance mechanism; workforce composition summary |
| 10(2)(c–e) indigenous/FPIC ≥ high | Verify FPIC processes for plots in/near customary areas; engage on active disputes | FPIC process documentation for affected plots; overlap check vs customary land claims; status note on relevant disputes |
| 10(2)(m) substantiated concerns ≥ medium | Screen supplier and its group against named grievances; require responses | Supplier statement on named grievances; corrective action plans where applicable |
| 10(2)(n) certification = supportive | Use certification data as supporting evidence, never as sole proof | Valid certificates (e.g. MSPO, RSPO) with scope, model (IP/SG/MB) and validity; certified unit list matched to supplying mills |

Escalation rule: a **high** rating upgrades related baseline items from *declaration-level* to *proof-level* (e.g. "labour compliance declaration" becomes "declaration + supporting records"). The escalation is recorded on the item.

## Merge and deduplication

1. Collect stream 1 (baseline) + stream 2 (add-ons) for the country/region × cycle.
2. Deduplicate identical documents; a merged item keeps **all** its justifications.
3. Each final item carries: document name; description/acceptance criteria; stream(s); justification links (requirement IDs and/or section ratings + rule IDs); escalation level; grouping (area of law or risk theme).
4. The checklist version is stamped with the generation time and the versions of every input (ratings, requirements, rules) — regenerating after a rating change produces a new checklist version; old versions remain readable.

## Output

- **In-app view:** grouped checklist with expandable justification per item ("requested because: section 10(2)(j) rated high on [date] — rule R-07; and legal requirement A1-03").
- **Exports:** docx (formatted, for sending context to suppliers) and xlsx (working list). Naming per the export convention (`06`/`07`). Every export is logged.

## Completeness guards

- The checklist cannot be generated as "final" while any of the ten sections is unsigned or any legal area has zero refined requirements — it generates as DRAFT with the gaps listed.
- A rating change or requirement change after generation flags the existing checklist **stale — regenerate**.
